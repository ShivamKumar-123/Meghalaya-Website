Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Meghalaya?

The best time to visit Meghalaya is from October to May when the weather is pleasant. However, if you want to experience the monsoon beauty and waterfalls at their peak, June to September is ideal.

How can I reach Meghalaya?

The nearest airport is Guwahati (Assam), about 130 km from Shillong. You can also reach by train to Guwahati and then take a taxi or bus to Meghalaya.

Do I need any permits to visit Meghalaya?

Indian citizens don't need any special permits. Foreign nationals may need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) for certain areas. Please check the latest requirements before traveling.

What are the must-visit places in Meghalaya?

Must-visit places include Living Root Bridges, Dawki River, Cherrapunji, Mawlynnong Village, Elephant Falls, and the Mawsmai Caves.
