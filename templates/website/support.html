Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Meghalaya?
The best time to visit Meghalaya is from October to May. The monsoon season (June-September) brings heavy rainfall, especially in Cherrapunji and Mawsynram. However, if you want to experience the lush green landscapes and waterfalls at their best, the post-monsoon period (October-November) is ideal.
How do I reach Meghalaya?
You can reach Meghalaya by air through Shillong Airport (Umroi) or Guwahati Airport (Assam). From Guwahati, Shillong is about 100 km by road (3-4 hours). Regular bus services and taxis are available from Guwahati to Shillong.
Is it safe to travel to Meghalaya?
Yes, Meghalaya is generally safe for tourists. The locals are friendly and hospitable. However, like any travel destination, it's advisable to take standard precautions, especially when trekking or visiting remote areas.
What are the must-visit places in Meghalaya?
Must-visit places include: Living Root Bridges in Cherrapunji, Dawki River, Mawlynnong Village (Asia's cleanest village), Elephant Falls, Umiam Lake, Mawsmai Caves, Nohkalikai Falls, and the capital city Shillong.
Do I need any permits to visit Meghalaya?
Indian nationals do not need any special permits to visit Meghalaya. Foreign nationals may need an Inner Line Permit (ILP) for certain restricted areas. It's best to check the latest requirements before your trip.
