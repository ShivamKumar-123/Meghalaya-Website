Our Story

Meghalaya Tourism was born from a deep love for the northeastern gem of India. Our journey began with a simple mission: to share the breathtaking beauty of the "Abode of Clouds" with the world.

From the living root bridges of Cherrapunji to the crystal-clear waters of Dawki River, we've explored every corner of this magical land. Now, we're here to guide you through the same incredible experiences.

Our team consists of local experts, passionate travelers, and nature enthusiasts who understand the true essence of Meghalaya. We believe in responsible tourism that benefits both travelers and local communities.
